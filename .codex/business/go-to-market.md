# Go-To-Market Plan

## Initial Market

Start in Cebu.

Reason:

```txt
Strong cafe culture
Local community advantage
Easier cafe outreach
Better manual validation
Good fit for student/young professional users
```

## Target Early Users

```txt
Students
Young professionals
Cafe hoppers
Content creators
Food reviewers
Remote workers
Study cafe users
```

## Initial Cafe Targets

```txt
Independent cafes
Small local cafe chains
Aesthetic cafes
Study cafes
Specialty coffee shops
Matcha/dessert cafes
```

## Launch Strategy

### Phase 1 — Seed Content

```txt
Manually add 30-50 cafes
Add sample menu products
Create initial posts
Invite friends/creators
```

### Phase 2 — Community Launch

```txt
Launch on Facebook
Launch on TikTok
Invite Cebu cafe reviewers
Run “Rank your favorite cafe drink” campaign
```

### Phase 3 — Cafe Outreach

```txt
Show cafe profile samples
Offer free claiming for early cafes
Collect feedback
Offer paid upgrades later
```

### Phase 4 — Monetization

```txt
Verified profiles
Featured placements
Analytics
Promo campaigns
```

## First Campaign Ideas

```txt
Top Spanish Latte in Cebu
Best Matcha in Cebu
Best Study Cafe Drink
Most Upranked Pastry
CafeRank Cebu Top 10
```

## Early Pitch to Cafes

> CafeRank helps cafes discover which products customers actually love. Instead of just getting likes on Instagram, cafes can see product rankings, customer feedback, and community-driven demand.
