# CafeRank Project Brief

## Project Name

CafeRank

## One-Liner

CafeRank is a social ranking app where people discover, post, comment, and uprank the best cafe products.

## Problem

People often want to know:

- What cafe should I visit?
- What drink should I order?
- Which pastry is worth trying?
- What cafe products are trending near me?
- Which cafe has the best matcha, latte, or dessert?

Current platforms mostly rank cafes as businesses, not the actual products people order.

## Solution

CafeRank lets users post specific cafe products and allows the community to uprank them.

Instead of saying:

> Espresso Bay is good.

Users can say:

> The Spanish Latte from Espresso Bay is ranked #3 in Cebu this week.

## Target Users

### Regular Users

People who like cafes, drinks, pastries, desserts, and aesthetic cafe content.

### Cafe Owners

Cafes that want more discovery, feedback, product visibility, and customer insight.

### Content Creators

Cafe reviewers and micro-influencers who want to build credibility around cafe recommendations.

## MVP Goal

Validate that users want to discover and rank cafe products through social posts.

## MVP Hook

> Find the most upranked cafe products near you.

## Core UX

The app should feel like Instagram, but the heart/like action is replaced with **Uprank**.
