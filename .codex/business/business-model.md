# Business Model

## Core Business Model

CafeRank should be free for regular users.

Revenue should come from cafes.

## Main Revenue Streams

```txt
Verified cafe profiles
Featured placements
Sponsored product campaigns
Cafe analytics dashboard
Vouchers and deals
Ranking badges
Product launch campaigns
```

## Free Cafe Page

Includes:

```txt
Listed cafe page
User-generated posts
Community ranking
Basic product visibility
```

## Basic Plan — ₱299/month

Includes:

```txt
Claim cafe page
Edit cafe info
Add official photos
Manage menu/products
```

## Growth Plan — ₱799/month

Includes:

```txt
Everything in Basic
Analytics dashboard
Post promos
View top products
Customer feedback summary
```

## Premium Plan — ₱1,499/month

Includes:

```txt
Everything in Growth
Featured placement credits
Competitor insights
Campaign reports
Ranking badge kit
```

## Sponsored Placement

Examples:

```txt
Featured Cafe of the Week
Featured Product
Sponsored Explore placement
New Drink Campaign
```

Possible pricing:

```txt
₱500–₱1,500 per week for small cafes
₱3,000–₱10,000 per campaign for bigger brands
```

## Analytics Value

Cafe owners can see:

```txt
Most upranked product
Most commented product
Average product rating
Customer sentiment
Trending products
Competitor comparison
```

## Business Rule

Users create demand and content.

Cafes pay for visibility, management, analytics, and conversion.
